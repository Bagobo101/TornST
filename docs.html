Overview

Torn Stock Alerts is a free, unofficial companion tool for the browser game Torn. It watches stock prices you choose and notifies you when they cross a price you set. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no backend server operated by this app, and no account system.

Two outside services are used, both directly from your own browser:

  • Torn's official API (api.torn.com) — for live stock prices, bank interest rates, your holdings, and your name, using your own API key.
  • Firebase Realtime Database — an optional shared price-history pool, so the price graphs fill in faster using data from every visitor, not just you.

Torn's developers have approved this website for publication and use. It is independently developed and operated, not an official Torn service.

Get started

Once Torn confirms your API key, this guide walks through the basic setup. The app only reads game data; it never makes trades or other in-game changes.

  1. Connect a key. Open the key menu in the app and paste a Torn API key. A Public Only key is enough for stock prices and alerts. Choose Build a key for this app if you also want bank rates, automatic holdings, and your player-name greeting.
  2. Choose stocks and set alerts. Check a stock to watch it, then enter a price threshold or a percentage move. The percentage baseline is the current price when you set it.
  3. Choose how often to check. In Settings, set the polling interval. The tab must stay open for live checks; background browser tabs may be throttled.
  4. Turn on notifications. Allow browser notifications from the app if you want alerts while you're looking at another tab.
  5. Back up your watchlist. Copy its backup code or display a QR code to transfer it to another device. Your settings and watchlist are otherwise saved only in this browser.

You can optionally sync owned shares with a key that includes user: stocks, load real historical prices from Tornsy, and set a cash budget for buy suggestions.

App guide

All the “?” help in the app leads here. These notes explain the controls and the information shown in each section.

The app is split into tabs: Stocks for watchlists and price checks, Portfolio for holdings and bank comparisons, Compare for relative price charts, Backup for moving your watchlist between devices, and Alerts for the alert log.

API key

A Public Only key is enough for stock prices and alerts. The app-specific key builder selects stock prices, bank rates, your stock holdings, and your basic profile name. Keys are stored on this device and sent only to Torn's API. Logging out removes the saved key but keeps your watchlist and history.

Watching stocks and reading the list

Check a stock to watch it. Fixed “Notify below/above” prices trigger alerts at those prices. A percentage drop or rise uses the current price as its baseline when set, and alerts once it moves by that percentage. Leave fields blank to skip them.

Use Compare stocks to overlay up to five stocks as percentage changes over the selected graph range. Each line starts at that stock's first available price in the range, so different history coverage can mean different start times.

“This session” is the low and high observed by live checks since this tab was opened; it resets in a new session. The graph can include this browser's history, shared history, and (if loaded) Tornsy history, so its range can differ from the session range. “% of this session” describes where the current price falls between the session low and high; it is not a forecast.

“Watched only” filters out unchecked stocks. “Since last visit” compares the current price with the last saved visit. A new low/high badge means this check set a new record in the app's recorded data. Investor benefits are estimates based on Torn's descriptions and the shares entered. Active benefits pay out in increments; passive benefits require holding the needed shares for seven days. Enter your average buy price to see profit/loss, and “Last collected” to track the next active payout.

When a watched stock is near its session low and you have entered a budget, the app can suggest an affordable share count. Owned shares near a session high may show a “consider selling” signal. These are informational heuristics, not financial advice.

Prices, graphs, and alerts

Prices are requested directly from Torn's official API using your key in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else for the price check. “Sync my holdings” makes an additional call using the user: stocks selection to read your share counts; this is optional, and you can enter “You own” manually instead.

The session high/low shows the range observed since you started tracking in this app, not the stock's full history. Each stock's line graph shows price points recorded by the app, not Torn's full historical chart. If shared history is enabled, visitors' price points can be combined; otherwise the graph uses this browser's checks (plus any real history you manually loaded from Tornsy). The “Latest point recorded” timestamp is the time of the newest point in the graph; it does not mean a new price was recorded at every check.

Select Expand graph beneath a stock's sparkline to open a larger, touch-friendly chart. Move across the chart or use the left and right arrow keys to inspect a timestamp, price, and change from the first plotted point; tap or click to pin a point. The chart uses the graph range selected in Settings.

Buy suggestions are simple heuristics based on where the price sits in the range recorded here, not financial advice. Torn's stock market can move against any pattern. Alerts fire once when a threshold is crossed and re-arm after the price moves back between thresholds. Closing the app tab pauses price checks.

Settings

  • Cash to invest: used only to estimate share counts for buy suggestions; nothing is spent automatically.
  • Check every: controls how often prices are checked while the tab is open. More frequent checks use more of Torn's request allowance.
  • Sort by: order the list by name, biggest recent movers, or watched stocks closest to an alert.
  • Compact numbers: abbreviates large totals (for example, $1.2M); per-share prices stay unabridged.
  • Graph range: changes how much history is displayed, not what the app tracks.
  • Notifications: asks the browser to show alert pop-ups; browser permission is required.
  • Load real price history: manually fetches roughly seven days of hourly public prices from Tornsy. It is not automatic.
  • Reset all app data: erases the saved key, watchlist, thresholds, portfolio entries, ranges, and alert history on this device. This cannot be undone.
  • Donate: opens the developer's Torn profile; donating is optional.

Holdings, backups, and alerts

Sync my holdings reads your share counts from Torn and fills in “You own.” It requires the user: stocks key selection; otherwise the app reports the issue and you can enter holdings manually.

In a watched stock's “Alerts & position” panel, choose a snooze duration to pause that stock's notifications and alert-log entries. Snoozing is local to that stock and saved with the watchlist; resume early at any time. Price thresholds still update while snoozed, so a threshold crossed during the pause won't send a delayed notification afterward.

Your watchlist is local to this browser. Copy a backup code or QR code to transfer it to another device. QR scanning requires camera permission and HTTPS.

Alert history lists the latest 50 price, percentage, session high/low, and dividend alerts; clearing the history does not change your alert settings.

Payout per increment is the cash amount paid each time an active investor benefit pays out. The app may suggest an amount based on Torn's description, but verify it in-game; this value feeds the income summary and “what to buy next” ranking.

API key usage

Your Torn API key is what lets this app read live data from Torn on your behalf. Here's exactly what it's used for and how it's handled.

Where it's stored

Your key is saved only in this browser's local storage, on this device. It is never sent to any server other than api.torn.com itself — not to this app's developer, not to Firebase, not anywhere else. Clearing your browser data or using "Log out" removes it.

What it's used to fetch

SelectionWhat it's used for
torn: stocksLive prices for every stock, shown in the list and used for alerts.
torn: bankCurrent city bank interest rates, shown in the bank comparator.
user: stocksYour own share holdings, to auto-fill "shares owned" instead of typing it in.
user: basicYour player name, only to show a "Welcome back" message.

A Public Only key covers prices, bank rates, and alerts — that's all most people need. The "Build a key for this app" button in the key menu creates a key with exactly these four selections and nothing more, rather than a broader key with access this app doesn't use.

What it's never used for

  • No trading, buying, selling, or any action is ever taken in Torn on your behalf.
  • Nothing about your key or account is transmitted to Firebase or any third party.
  • No background automation of any in-game action — this app only ever reads published data.

Data & sharing

The price graphs on each stock can pull from two extra sources beyond your own browser's history, in addition to real historical data:

Shared price history (Firebase)

Every browser using this app can contribute price checks to a shared pool, so graphs fill in faster than any one person could alone. Only a timestamp and a price are ever written — never your name, your key, your holdings, or any other identifying information. The database's rules enforce this at the server level: any submission with extra fields is rejected outright, and nothing outside the shared price-history path is readable or writable by anyone.

Real historical prices (Tornsy)

The "Load real price history" button in Settings fetches historical prices from Tornsy.com, a free, public, community-run API that requires no key. This only happens when you click that button — nothing is fetched automatically or on a schedule. It's purely reading already-published price data.

What stays local only

  • Your API key, watchlist, alert thresholds, and avg buy prices
  • Your display preferences (theme, sort order, compact numbers)
  • Your alert history log

None of the above is ever shared, synced, or visible to anyone else using this app.
